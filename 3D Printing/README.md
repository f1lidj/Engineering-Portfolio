# 3D Printing & Rapid Prototyping

This section documents hands-on additive-manufacturing work used to support **RC-aircraft development and experimental test hardware**.

## My Work

I used 3D printing as a rapid-prototyping tool to produce and iterate functional parts, adapting designs around practical constraints such as mass, stiffness, manufacturability, assembly and hardware integration.

The work complements my analytical projects with direct experience in **design-for-manufacture, iterative prototyping and physical integration**.
