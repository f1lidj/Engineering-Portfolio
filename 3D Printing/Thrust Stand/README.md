# 3D-Printed Thrust-Stand Hardware

This work supported the development of a **static propulsion test stand** used for experimental thrust and torque measurements.

## My Work

I used additive manufacturing to produce and iterate mechanical components around the test setup, with attention to sensor placement, hardware interfaces, stiffness, assembly and ease of modification.

It was part of a broader hands-on activity combining mechanical prototyping, instrumentation and propulsion testing.
