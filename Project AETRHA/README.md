# Project AETHRA — Fixed-Wing UAV

AETHRA was a fixed-wing UAV developed within **Fly Mi – Politecnico di Milano**, including development activity for the **Air Cargo Challenge 2024**.

## My Role

**Head of Electric Propulsion Department**

Across AETHRA and NYX, I led a **12-member propulsion team** responsible for translating aircraft requirements into a flight-ready electric propulsion system.

My work included:

- propulsion-system sizing and architecture;
- motor, propeller and battery screening and selection;
- battery characterisation and high-power electrical integration;
- static and wind-tunnel propulsion testing;
- ESC, wiring, connector and cooling integration;
- hardware troubleshooting and aircraft-level propulsion integration;
- technical coordination of the propulsion team.

The combined development activity screened 20+ motors, 50+ battery configurations and hundreds of motor–propeller combinations, contributing to lower system mass and increased endurance.
