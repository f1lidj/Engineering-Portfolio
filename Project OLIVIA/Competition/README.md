# O.L.I.V.I.A. — Competition Deployment

This section documents the final deployment of **O.L.I.V.I.A.** at the IMechE UAS Challenge 2025 after a full design, manufacturing, integration and flight-test programme.

## My Responsibility

As **Chief Technology Officer**, I coordinated technical readiness for competition operations, including aircraft configuration, system verification, field troubleshooting, flight readiness and coordination between the technical departments.

The programme finished **3rd overall among 42 international teams**, received the **Safety Award** and was the highest-finishing Italian team.
