# Filippo Coacci — Aeronautical Engineering Portfolio

Selected engineering work in **UAV development, flight testing, electric propulsion, aircraft design, experimental aerodynamics, CFD, systems integration and prototyping**.

## Selected Experience

- **Project O.L.I.V.I.A.** — Chief Technology Officer for a 50+ member UAV programme; technical coordination, systems integration, verification, flight testing and competition deployment. The team placed 3rd overall at the IMechE UAS Challenge 2025 and received the Safety Award.
- **Projects NYX & AETHRA** — Head of Electric Propulsion, leading a 12-member team through propulsion sizing, battery and motor–propeller selection, bench and wind-tunnel testing, troubleshooting and aircraft integration.
- **KIRA** — Team Leader for the preliminary design of a long-endurance ISR / Counter-UAS aircraft, covering aircraft-level integration, mission architecture, sizing, payload integration, performance and preliminary structural work.
- **Pipistrel Flight Test Campaign** — Team Leader and on-board Flight Test Engineer for a structured manned-aircraft test campaign covering performance, stalls and longitudinal dynamics.
- **Experimental Aerodynamics** — Wind-tunnel research on spoileron effectiveness for an unconventional swept-wing configuration, including test execution, data analysis and publication.
- **Other Aerospace Projects** — Supersonic inlet CFD and preliminary design of an air-launched orbital system.
- **Thrust & Torque Test Stand** — Development and integration of an Arduino-based propulsion test system with load-cell acquisition, calibration, user interface and PC-side logging.
- **3D Printing** — Hands-on additive-manufacturing work supporting RC-aircraft and experimental-hardware prototyping.
